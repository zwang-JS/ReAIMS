Personal Information Collection Statements (PICS)

The personal data provided by you will be used by the University for the purposes directly related to the admission, registration, teaching and administration of your studies, and for the provision of student services.

Your personal data may be transferred to and accessed by other offices and departments of the University, and to external bodies where such transfer is required or permitted by law. The University will not disclose your personal data to any third party for direct marketing purposes without your consent.

You have the right to request access to and correction of your personal data held by the University. Requests should be made in writing to the IT Service Desk. A fee may be charged for the processing of a data access request.
